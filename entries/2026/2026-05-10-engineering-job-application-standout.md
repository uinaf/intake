# How to Make Your Engineering Job Application Stand Out

Lee Robinson's advice from reviewing hundreds of engineering resumes/applications:

- Keep the resume to one page; link out to a website if more space is needed.
- A thoughtful personal website immediately differentiates candidates from most applications.
- If linking X/Twitter, clean it up first; reviewers may look.
- Link GitHub, but avoid noisy profile READMEs full of badges/images; make code and projects easy to inspect.
- Customize applications to the company; college coursework matters less for startups than for ATS-heavy FAANG-style funnels.
- Show AI/agent literacy on the resume and in projects; engineering roles increasingly expect it.
- Take LinkedIn seriously because it is still commonly shared internally.
- Show unique taste, strengths, interests, and writing; people want signals that you'd be interesting and thoughtful to work with.
- Do not let AI write the final cover letter/resume text. Use it for ideation, but write the actual prose by hand to avoid obvious AI phrasing.
- No photos on resumes; use linked profiles/sites for that.
- Prefer quality over quantity: three strong, thoughtful projects beat a wall of AI-slop projects.

**Take:** Strong hiring signal is not more content; it is compressed taste, proof of craft, and credible AI-era judgment. Cut cruft, make the best work easy to evaluate, and make the human behind the resume visible without turning the resume into a scrapbook.
