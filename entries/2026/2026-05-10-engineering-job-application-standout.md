Lee Robinson's notes from reviewing hundreds of engineering applications: stand out with compressed taste and easy-to-inspect work, not more pages of content.

## Key takeaways

- **One page**: Keep the resume to a page and link a site if you need more room. A thoughtful personal site differentiates immediately.
- **Clean links**: Tidy X if you link it. On GitHub, skip badge-heavy READMEs and make code easy to inspect.
- **Company fit**: Customize to the company. Coursework matters less at startups than in ATS-heavy FAANG funnels.
- **AI literacy**: Show agent and AI fluency on the resume and in projects; roles increasingly expect it.
- **Human prose**: Use AI for ideation, but write the final resume and cover letter yourself to avoid obvious AI phrasing.
- **Quality over volume**: Three strong projects beat a wall of slop. No photos on the resume; use linked profiles for that.
