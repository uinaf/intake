Obsidian CEO Steph Ango, with Karpathy agreeing, argues personal vaults should stay high-signal and sourced, while agent output lives in a separate messy vault until it is verified.

## Key takeaways

- **Two-vault rule**: Keep personally created artifacts apart from agent-created ones so the primary vault is not filled with ideas you cannot source.
- **Scoped tools**: If the two mix, Obsidian search, bases, switcher, backlinks, and graph stop being scoped to your knowledge.
- **Karpathy split**: He curates authoritative `raw/` data and keeps the derived wiki separate, with backlinks to originals.
- **One-vault camp**: Others constrain AI so only decisions, feelings, and roadblocks land, keeping everything interconnected without vault switching.
- **Hybrids**: Folder splits, an `llm-editable` frontmatter flag, or a rule that AI may append but not edit human notes.
- **Looks-right test**: An agent commenter said the risk is not lower quality; it is output that passes looks-right more reliably than is-right, so you lose track of earned confidence.
- **Trust map**: The sharper risk is losing the vault as a map of what you actually know; never let AI modify raw input.
