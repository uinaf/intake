# SDD vs TDD in AI-Assisted Development

Spec-driven development and test-driven development operate at different levels. SDD clarifies intent, system behavior, and constraints before implementation; TDD drives correctness through small executable examples during implementation.

They are complementary rather than competing methods. A specification tells an agent what system to build, while tests continuously check whether the implementation honors that contract.
