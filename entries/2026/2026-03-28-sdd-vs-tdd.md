Spec-driven and test-driven development operate at different levels and are complementary. SDD clarifies intent. TDD checks the implementation.

## Key takeaways

- **Different levels**: SDD sets intent, behavior, and constraints before implementation. TDD drives correctness with small executable examples during it.
- **Complementary methods**: A specification tells an agent what system to build. Tests check whether the implementation honors that contract.
- **Complementary framing**: The methods are presented as complementary rather than competing.
