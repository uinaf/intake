Martin Fowler argues harnesses will become the new service templates, and that the hard work is designing environments, feedback loops, and control systems rather than generating code.

## Key takeaways

- **Environment as job**: On OpenAI's 1M+ LOC project, the team's main job was designing the environment, not writing code.
- **Garbage-collection agents**: Agents can detect architectural violations and code decay.
- **Struggle as signal**: When agents struggle, treat it as missing tools, guardrails, or docs, and have the agent write the fix.
- **Retrofit and stacks**: Legacy codebases will be hard to retrofit. Teams may converge on fewer AI-friendly stacks.
