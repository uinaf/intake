A 2026 infrastructure brief that names four capabilities agentic development needs, and why existing CI/CD is too slow and coarse for agent-driven workflows.

## Key takeaways

- **Isolated sandboxes**: Each task needs its own isolated sandbox.
- **Sub-100ms start**: Startup under 100ms rules out traditional VMs and most Kubernetes approaches.
- **API lifecycle**: Sandbox lifetime must be API-driven rather than ticket- or pipeline-driven.
- **MCP-native control**: Environment control should be MCP-native; existing CI/CD is called insufficient for agent-driven development.
