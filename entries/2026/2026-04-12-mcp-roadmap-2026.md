Worth reading before overcommitting to today's MCP deployment shape: transport and discovery are still moving, and remote sessionful transport is the scaling pain.

## Key takeaways

- **Moving transport**: Transport and discovery are still moving; worth reading before overcommitting to today's MCP deployment shape.
- **Sessionful scaling**: The scaling pain is sessionful transport; once MCP goes remote, load balancing and state management stop being boring details.
- **Still immature**: Treat MCP as important and still immature.
