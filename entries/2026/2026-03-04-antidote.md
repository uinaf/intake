Vicki Boykis: if you love building and the process matters as much as the result, feeling in a slump is normal. The world is telling you your thinking process is extraneous.

## Key takeaways

- **Process matters**: In engineering, the journey is the destination; the working system in production is the reward.
- **Inefficiency**: Unstick yourself by doing things that are not the most efficient.
- **Hands-on**: Build from scratch, read paper books, reason on an envelope, join building Discords.
- **Agency**: Having agency is one of the most important things a creator can have; give it to yourself.
