If you love building and the process matters as much as the result, it's normal to feel in a slump. The world is telling you your thinking process is extraneous, unnecessary, must be commoditized and compressed.

But you are multidimensional. You need room to touch the code, to explore, to rise above the local minima. In engineering, the journey is the destination. The working system in production is our reward.

Prescription: unstick yourself. Do stuff that is not the most efficient.

- Build something from scratch with your bare hands and your brain
- Read programming books on dead trees
- Reason through something on the back of an envelope
- Join Discords where people are actively building, reviewing PRs, building community
- Write a blog post, mentor a colleague, answer an email from a budding engineer
- Create a private repo and merge to main without a PR
- Pick apart the transformer down to the attention mechanism, down to the RoPE embeddings. Explain it back to yourself.

> Having agency is one of the most important things a creator can have. Give the gift of it to yourself. It's still there, waiting for you.
