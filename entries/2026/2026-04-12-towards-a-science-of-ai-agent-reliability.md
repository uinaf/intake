Proposes twelve reliability metrics across consistency, robustness, predictability, and safety, evaluated on 14 agentic models. Capability gains have yielded only modest reliability improvements.

## Key takeaways

- **Twelve metrics**: Concrete measures across consistency, robustness, predictability, and safety.
- **Fourteen models**: The metrics are evaluated against 14 agentic models, not a single flagship.
- **Modest reliability**: Recent capability gains produce only modest reliability gains.
- **Harness discipline**: That gap is the empirical case for reliability engineering at the harness layer, not just model selection.
