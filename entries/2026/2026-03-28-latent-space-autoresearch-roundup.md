A Latent Space roundup on autoresearch and recursive self-improvement, covering fragile agent loops, code-review gains, and a shift from implementation to verification.

## Key takeaways

- **Fragile loops**: 700 changes yielded 20 transferable improvements and an 11% speedup, but loops depend heavily on harness and infra, not just the model.
- **Model differences**: GPT-5.4 struggles with continuous looping that Opus 4.6 handles well. Robust looping and interruption handling matter.
- **Review lift**: Anthropic's code-review plugin went from 16% to 54% of PRs with meaningful comments and under 1% false positives.
- **Bottleneck shift**: Implementation is cheaper. Review, verification, and governance are the constraint. Users mix models by role rather than seeking one best model.
