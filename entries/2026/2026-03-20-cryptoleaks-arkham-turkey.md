# CryptoLeaks Case No. 13 — Arkham in Turkey

## Key Players
- **Arkham Intelligence** — US-based blockchain analytics / crypto exchange company (HQ: Dominican Republic). Investors include Sam Altman, Peter Thiel, Tim Draper, Joe Lonsdale. Thiel resigned from board after earlier CryptoLeaks investigation.
- **Miguel Morel** — Arkham CEO, US citizen, 21 when he founded the company. Ties to Leverage Research (cult-like org) and Sam Bankman-Fried.
- **Efe Buken** — Arkham's "Head of Turkey," central figure in 31 covertly recorded videos.

## Core Allegations (from recorded conversations)
1. **Regulatory capture of SPK (Turkey's SEC equivalent):** Buken claims a former student of his works at SPK. Says he told the official draft regs were "bullshit" and ~40% were changed based on his feedback — specifically to allow foreign entities (Arkham) to operate exchanges in Turkey.
2. **Confidential documents obtained from SPK:** Sealed government-stamped docs on competitor companies allegedly passed to Arkham. Buken claims Morel was personally present at the handover meeting.
3. **Bribery / "incentivizing" regulators:** Buken uses the word "incentivize" regarding government officials. Potential FCPA violation (up to $250K per violation, 20 years prison).
4. **Galatasaray sponsorship (~€3.7M):** Not about football — Buken says the goal was converting Turkey's massive gambling population into crypto traders on Arkham's platform.
5. **Sex workers for Galatasaray coach:** Buken claims he arranged sex workers for coach Okan Buruk to secure player access for a promo video, paid from a "black ops" off-the-books budget.
6. **Tiered comms to hide crimes:** Slack for official business, encrypted messaging for sensitive topics, in-person meetings for anything criminal. All reported up to CEO Morel.
7. **Outpaced Coinbase:** Coinbase spent 2 years failing to enter Turkey. Arkham allegedly did it in ~5 months through Buken's connections.

## Background
- Arkham's debut was a 2021 disinformation report against DFINITY/ICP token (co-published by NYT), allegedly produced to order for competing investors.
- CEO Morel and CTO were with Sam Bankman-Fried in the Bahamas the day before FTX collapsed.
- Mysterious $275K payment from Arkham to FTX's PR firm around the time of the disinfo report.
- After CryptoLeaks published a video summary (Dec 2025, 15M+ views), Buken's LinkedIn was scrubbed of all Arkham references.

## Legal Exposure
- US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA): bribing foreign officials, up to 20 years federal prison
- Turkish law: bribery of public servants, imprisonment
- Morel as US citizen + Arkham as US company = full FCPA jurisdiction
