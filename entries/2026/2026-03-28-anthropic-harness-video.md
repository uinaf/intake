A 16-minute companion video to Anthropic's long-running harness post, with live demos of the same planner-generator-evaluator setup.

## Key takeaways

- **Live demos**: The video shows the Dutch museum 3D room leap, the AI-composed DAW, and a solo-versus-harness game-maker comparison.
- **Model change**: Opus 4.6 eliminated context resets and sprint decomposition. A 1M-token window with compaction replaced resets.
- **Harness weight**: For long-running complex tasks, harness design is as important as the model itself.
- **Cost contrast**: The DAW took about 4 hours and $125. The game maker was $9 solo versus $200 with the full harness, with a large quality gap.
