Matt Pocock: process has never been more important, and skills are the best way to bundle processes for agents. Five daily skills cover grill, PRD, issues, TDD, and improve.

## Key takeaways

- **Grill-me**: A 30-45 minute design tree of questions before coding.
- **PRD path**: Conversation becomes a GitHub-issue PRD; implementation decisions stay non-prescriptive.
- **Tracer bullets**: `prd-to-issues` cuts thin vertical slices with blocking relationships for parallel agents.
- **TDD**: Confirm interfaces and behaviors, then red-green-refactor; the most consistent way he improved agent output.
- **Deep modules**: `/improve-my-codebase` hunts shallow modules and spawns competing interface designs.
- **Human-like process**: Treat agents like humans with weird constraints; skills are language-agnostic and invoked manually.
