A dual-layer open-source harness: Crew for autonomous agent delegation, Flow for event-driven deterministic control.

## Key takeaways

- **Crew layer**: Handles autonomous agent delegation.
- **Flow layer**: Event-driven deterministic control with branching and shared Pydantic state.
- **Mixed execution**: The clearest open-source example of mixing autonomous and scripted execution in the same harness.
