Dax says LLMs turbocharge three existing engineering problems and the quality loss is not even traded for speed.

## Key takeaways

- **Low bar features**: Easy-to-prompt features drop the bar for what ships; prototypes are not worth more than product thinking.
- **Refactoring dies**: Models tolerate hackiness, so the impulse to fix the original design disappears.
- **Cleanup deprioritized**: Agents pull toward the next feature; more value is in fixing what exists.
- **No speed gain**: Normal pace, just with more technical debt.
- **Friction and discipline**: The tool that removes all friction also removes all discipline.
