Chayenne Zhao argues harness engineering is existing software-engineering principles rebranded, illustrated by an SGLang community multi-agent Q&A system.

## Key takeaways

- **Rebranded principles**: Separation of concerns, single responsibility, docs-as-code, and shift-left constraints are presented as the real content of harness engineering.
- **Partitioned experts**: One omniscient agent failed. Domain-specific agents with a structural routing table improved answers more than model upgrades.
- **Repo as knowledge**: Verbal agreements do not exist for agents. All knowledge lives in the repo.
- **Open question**: If models keep scaling, they may eventually build their own environments. Naming still creates searchability even if the patterns later go stale.
