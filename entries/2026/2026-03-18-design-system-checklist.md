Arda Karaçizmeli's open-source checklist for planning, building, and growing a design system: language, foundations, 30 components, and maintenance.

## Key takeaways

- **Design language**: Vision, principles, tone, glossary, brand assets, plus accessibility, writing, microcopy, and i18n guidelines.
- **Foundations**: Color, layout, type, elevation, motion, and iconography. Each foundation has contrast, naming, and usage rules.
- **Component states**: Every component lists states and features (loading, disabled, error, keyboard a11y) plus links to Reshaped, Atlassian, Material, Polaris, and Spectrum.
- **Maintenance**: Docs, local-pattern rules, changelog and roadmap, community channels, and contribution templates.
- **Quality bar**: Use it to check whether a system's tokens, states, and processes are actually defined.
