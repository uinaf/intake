MCP tool annotations matter less as labels than as a shared risk vocabulary the harness can reason about. They are hints, not enforcement.

## Key takeaways

- **Shared vocabulary**: The useful shift is a risk language the harness can act on, not the annotation fields themselves.
- **Lethal combo**: Private data access plus untrusted content plus external communication is the combination single-tool reviews miss.
- **Hints only**: Annotations do not enforce policy. The harness still has to do the hard part.
