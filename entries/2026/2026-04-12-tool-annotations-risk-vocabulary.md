- The useful contribution is not the annotations themselves, it is the shift to a shared risk vocabulary the harness can reason about.
- The lethal combo is private data access plus untrusted content plus external communication. Single-tool reviews miss this all the time.
- Annotations are hints, not enforcement. The harness still has to do the hard part.
