The first large-scale empirical fault taxonomy for agent systems, from 13,602 issues across 40 open-source repositories.

## Key takeaways

- **Taxonomy size**: 37 fault types, 13 symptom classes, and 12 root-cause categories.
- **Issue corpus**: Derived from 13,602 issues across 40 open-source repositories.
- **Harness mismatch**: Most failures come from mismatches between probabilistically generated artifacts and deterministic interface constraints. This is a structural harness problem, not a model capability problem.
