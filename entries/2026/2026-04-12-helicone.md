An open-source LLM observability proxy (YC W23) with a one-line integration for cost tracking, token monitoring, session tracing, and prompt versioning.

## Key takeaways

- **Pricing database**: Largest open-source pricing database, covering 300+ models.
- **AI Gateway**: Request routing and caching with zero-code changes.
- **Compliance and hosting**: SOC 2 and GDPR compliant, self-hostable via Docker.
- **Cost complement**: The natural complement to execution-tracing tools when cost attribution is the primary concern.
