Unified proxy and SDK that routes to 100+ LLM providers behind a single OpenAI-compatible interface, with retry, fallback, and cost tracking.

## Key takeaways

- **Unified provider interface**: Routes to 100+ LLM providers behind a single OpenAI-compatible interface.
- **Retry and fallback**: A Router handles retry and fallback across deployments, plus per-project cost and rate-limit tracking.
- **OTEL callbacks**: Includes OpenTelemetry callback integrations.
- **Provider resilience**: The right infrastructure layer when a harness needs automatic failover on 429/500 errors, budget guardrails, or swapping models without touching orchestration code.
