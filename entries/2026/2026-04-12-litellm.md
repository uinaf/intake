## Why it matters
Unified proxy and SDK that routes to 100+ LLM providers behind a single OpenAI-compatible interface, with a Router handling retry/fallback across deployments, per-project cost and rate-limit tracking, and OTEL callback integrations. The right infrastructure layer when your harness needs provider resilience (automatic failover on 429/500 errors), budget guardrails, or the ability to swap models without touching orchestration code.
