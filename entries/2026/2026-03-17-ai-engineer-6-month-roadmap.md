A 10,000+ word guide argues that AI engineering is not ML research. It builds useful systems on existing models through APIs, prompts, retrieval, automation, and real products.

## Key takeaways

- **Months 1-2**: Python, git, APIs, FastAPI, then prompting, structured outputs, tool calling, streaming, cost, and prompt-injection awareness.
- **Month 3 RAG**: Embeddings, chunking (~250 tokens, 10-20% overlap), vector DBs, reranking, citations, LlamaIndex versus LangChain.
- **Month 4 agents**: Build a loop from scratch; use agents only when steps are unpredictable; add eval harnesses.
- **Month 5 production**: Docker, queues, auth, observability, prompt versioning, caching, and cost controls.
- **Month 6 fork**: Product engineer (UX, Vercel AI SDK) or applied ML (fine-tuning, inference).
- **Canon**: Anthropic's Building Effective Agents, OpenAI's Practical Guide, Instructor, DeepEval, Langfuse.
