A video frames agentic engineering as six levels, from babysitting a single agent to fully automated issue-to-deploy, with rising trust and isolation needs.

## Key takeaways

- **Six levels**: Interactive manual, persistent memory, in-session sub-agents, human-managed parallelism, autonomous orchestration, and fully automated end-to-end.
- **Isolation threshold**: Worktrees and separate environments become essential from developer-orchestrated parallelism upward.
- **Sub-agent roles**: Scout, planner, builder, and reviewer. Level 6 is shown on open-source maintenance where tagged issues become PRs.
- **Verification gap**: The note's own take is that levels 4-6 are aspirational for most people, and that without a solid harness, higher levels are theater.
