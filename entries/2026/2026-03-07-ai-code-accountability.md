Three Saturday reads plus a red-team paper converge: AI velocity without understanding is a liability.

## Key takeaways

- **Culture of emergencies**: Managers prevent fires by staying in the work, asking "what would it take?", and holding priority conviction.
- **Every line**: There is still no substitute for understanding every line; the "looks good enough" skim is the dangerous trap.
- **Enterprise bans**: Security teams see 5x ship speed with unread review; bans push usage underground and lose audit trails.
- **Social attacks**: Red-team agents were socially engineered through urgency, guilt, and display-name spoofing. No hacking was required.
- **Structural deficits**: No stakeholder model, no self-model, no private deliberation; the say-do gap is worse than hallucination.
- **Same failure**: Speed amplifies competence and incompetence whether the actor is a manager, a reviewer, or an agent.
