A machine-readable catalogue of 100+ named agentic patterns covering evaluation, orchestration, context, specs, self-improvement, and safety.

## Key takeaways

- **Evaluation patterns**: Critic-style review, anti-reward-hacking graders, spec-as-test loops, and rich feedback treat ground truth as more important than perfect prompts.
- **Orchestration patterns**: Planner-worker splits, initializer-maintainer roles, factory-over-assistant, and oracle-and-worker multi-model setups.
- **Context patterns**: Anxiety management, auto-compaction, minimization, cross-cycle relays, and agents writing their own notes.
- **Safety patterns**: Hook-based guardrails, deterministic security scanning, and human approval for high-risk functions.
- **How to use**: Each pattern page has a problem, solution, examples, and references, indexed via llms.txt.
