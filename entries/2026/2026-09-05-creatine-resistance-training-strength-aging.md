Nicholas Fabiano highlights creatine alongside lifting as a way to improve strength with age. His post points to a review on creatine monohydrate as one component of addressing sarcopenia, or age-related muscle loss.

## Key takeaways

- **Combined approach**: The post emphasizes creatine together with resistance training, rather than presenting supplementation as a replacement for exercise.
- **Research source**: The author links the review [Creatine monohydrate supplementation: one tool in the toolbox for treating sarcopenia](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/30653495.2026.2727350) and identifies himself as a contributor to the paper led by Darren Candow.
- **Evidence scope**: This is a brief summary of a review, not a report of a new trial. The post does not specify dosage, effect sizes, or individual suitability, and the full paper was not accessible for this capture.
