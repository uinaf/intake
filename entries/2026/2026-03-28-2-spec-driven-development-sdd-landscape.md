A map of 30+ agentic coding frameworks sorted into four layers rather than treated as competitors.

## Key takeaways

- **Four layers**: Spec frameworks, planning systems, execution agents, and AI IDEs that fold planning and execution into the editor.
- **Structured shift**: The claimed shift is from prompt-to-code to spec-plan-tasks-code, on the argument that agents do better on structured tasks.
- **Specs as interface**: The strong thesis is that specs become the primary human interface and implementations become generated artifacts.
- **Cautious pairing**: ThoughtWorks flags missing spec-quality standards, non-reproducible generation, spec drift, and no settled authority between spec and code.
