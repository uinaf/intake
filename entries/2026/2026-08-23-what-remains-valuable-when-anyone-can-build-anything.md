Eero Alvar argues one-person companies get cheaper because AI is synthetic labor without other minds. That efficiency becomes the baseline, so value moves to judgment inside the firm and trust outside it.

## Key takeaways

- **Human leverage is lossy**: Hiring or splitting equity buys time, then burns it on context transfer, misaligned objectives, and vision drift. One mind plus agents skips those coordination taxes.
- **Not “anyone can build anything”**: The floor of execution rose. The ceiling is still choosing the right thing. AI cannot hold downside or liability, so the chain of delegation ends in a human.
- **Efficiency is not advantage**: What one person can now do, everyone can do. Maximal one-person efficiency becomes the new default, not a moat.
- **Complements appreciate**: Cheap cognition makes selection more valuable. Cheap content and marketing make attention and trust more valuable.
- **Where to spend**: Invest scarce time in field competence (judgment under leverage) and in audiences, network, and community (forms of trust that cannot be manufactured).
