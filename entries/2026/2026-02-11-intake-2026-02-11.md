SaaS value is shifting from UI to API. Three survival paths: go headless, make the UI irreplaceable, or go agent-to-agent.

## Key takeaways

- **Apps are dead**: Directionally right but overstates the timeline; the agent-to-agent path is most interesting and least developed, and every SaaS company should audit its API through an agent's eyes.
- **Linear work**: Coordination is the bottleneck, not execution, but Intent-style spec-driven development is basically waterfall with better branding.
- **Cursor scale**: Impressive, but all compiled-language projects that sidestep JS monorepos; the real insight is a coordinator distributing non-overlapping work.
- **Social singularity**: Social disruption front-runs technical capability; panic itself becomes the mechanism, even if MMLU and tokens/dollar are weak capability measures.
- **Something big**: Directionally correct compounding, but the essay overhypes the timeline and undersells adoption messiness.
- **Delegation gap**: Anthropic's report is the most grounded so far; developers use AI in 60% of work but fully delegate only 0-20%.
- **Procrastination**: Neuroscience framing elevates standard advice; the ambivalence distinction is the real gem.
- **Security and Stripe**: Isolation matters for group-facing bots; Stripe's 1000+ PRs/week from unattended agents is the most concrete production evidence yet.
